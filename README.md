## 🎮 About the Game

ShoreTiles is a single-player tile-placement strategy and settlement defense game developed by Voxel Studios and released on September 3, 2026. Players expand an island around a lighthouse, manage resources, build defenses, and survive escalating attacks from creatures emerging from the sea.

It supports resource management, tile placement, settlement construction, fog control, lighthouse protection, and military preparation while preserving the game's core day-and-night strategy.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Resources** — Prevents resource shortages while building farms, houses, walls, barracks, and other settlement structures.
- **Unlimited Trader Funds** — Provides effectively unlimited spending power when purchasing island tiles from the trader.
- **Tile Placement Assist** — Removes common placement limitations to make it easier to expand and shape the island.
- **Freeze Day Cycle** — Pauses the day-night progression so players can plan construction and defenses without the night arriving.
- **Reveal Fog** — Reveals areas hidden by the fog and reduces the need to rely on braziers for visibility.
- **Instant Construction** — Speeds up settlement development so available buildings and defensive structures can be established immediately.
- **Permanent Placement Recovery** — Provides a safety option for experimenting with permanent tile placements where supported by the trainer.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Protect the Lighthouse** — Helps keep the central lighthouse safe during attacks from creatures rising from the sea.
- **Enhanced Military Camps** — Supports stronger military preparations when combining camps, dividing troops, and defending multiple fronts.
- **Reduced Enemy Pressure** — Lowers the impact of escalating nighttime threats for a more relaxed survival-focused playthrough.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

ShoreTiles focuses on strategic island management rather than direct character movement. Use the mouse and camera controls to inspect the island, review possible tile placements, and position buildings, walls, camps, and other structures. The trainer's placement and fog options are intended to make planning and navigation across the expanding settlement easier.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional character inventory. Progress is built around a randomized tile deck, resources, and purchases from the trader. Resource and trader-related options help maintain the materials and funds needed to expand the island, construct buildings, and prepare for the next night.

### World & Environment

During the day, draw and place island tiles permanently, connect terrain, expand the settlement, gather resources, and build houses, farms, barracks, walls, and other structures around the lighthouse. Braziers can push back the fog, while exploration may uncover relics, new structures, blessings, curses, and additional dangers. The reveal and placement options help examine the procedurally generated island and test different layouts.

### Combat & Weapons

Nighttime turns the settlement into a defensive battle. Creatures emerge from the depths and advance toward the lighthouse and the territory surrounding it. Prepare by combining military camps into stronger armies, dividing troops to cover multiple fronts, using boats to reinforce separated areas, and building walls and other defenses. The lighthouse protection, enhanced military, and reduced enemy pressure options support testing defensive formations without removing the game's core combat structure.
